* Assignments 15%
   * A short programming assignment 7% (group)
   * Writing paper reviews 8% (individual, two reviews, one page each)
* Two in-class tests 30% (individual, closed book, 45 minutes each)
* Leading paper discussion and class participation 10%
* Course project 45% (group)
   * Project proposal 5%
   * Project presentation 20%
   * Project report 20%
(65% is the minimum passing grade for Master’s students, 75% is the minimum passing grade for PhD students.)
